Domain and Renewal Policy

All ARTE website packages include first-year website hosting, website deployment and complete website handover. Domain registration is billed separately unless specifically included in your quotation. The domain will be registered in the client's name. Hosting and domain renewals after the first year are the client's responsibility. Ongoing maintenance, updates and additional technical support are available separately.

Hosting is matched to the website's technical requirements: standard HTML, CSS and JavaScript websites use static hosting, while backend functionality, databases, appointment systems and advanced forms may require separately quoted services. Business email, paid plugins, premium integrations and third-party subscriptions are not included unless specified in your quotation.
