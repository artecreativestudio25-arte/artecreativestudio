Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · ARTE Creative Studio, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

1. Overview

ARTE Creative Studio respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and business information you share when visiting our website or submitting an enquiry.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit our project enquiry form, we collect only the details you voluntarily provide:

  • Full name and business or clinic name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Business category, requested service and approximate budget range
  • Project brief details you choose to share

Please do not include sensitive patient health records or confidential medical histories in general website enquiry forms.

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted is used strictly to review your requirements, prepare a custom proposal and communicate regarding potential creative or digital services. We do not sell, rent or trade your contact details to third parties.

4. Form Processing & Third-Party Tools

This static website loads typography from Google Fonts and, when configured in production, routes enquiry submissions through a designated form-processing provider (such as Formspree) subject to their respective security and privacy practices.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of enquiry details you have shared with us at any time by contacting us at artecreativestudio25@gmail.com.

6. Contact

For privacy questions regarding ARTE Creative Studio, write to artecreativestudio25@gmail.com · Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
