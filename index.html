Domain and Renewal Policy

All ARTE website packages include first-year website hosting, website deployment and complete website handover. Domain registration is billed separately unless specifically included in your quotation. The domain will be registered in the client's name. Hosting and domain renewals after the first year are the client's responsibility. Ongoing maintenance, updates and additional technical support are available separately.

Hosting is matched to the website's technical requirements: standard HTML, CSS and JavaScript websites use static hosting, while backend functionality, databases, appointment systems and advanced forms may require separately quoted services. Business email, paid plugins, premium integrations and third-party subscriptions are not included unless specified in your quotation.

OUR COMMITMENT

Healthcare communication principles.

Healthcare communication carries responsibility. We never guarantee patient outcomes or invent medical claims, and every deliverable respects clinical ethics.

01

Medical accuracy

Content is grounded in verified clinical context without exaggeration or unverified claims.

02

Doctor approval

Educational posts, video scripts and clinical pages are reviewed and approved by the practitioner before publishing.

03

Patient privacy

Strict care around patient confidentiality across clinic photography, enquiries and digital workflows.

04

Appropriate consent

Written consent protocols for any real clinical spaces, staff appearances or patient-facing media.

05

Ethical visual communication

Calm, respectful imagery and clear typography—free from fear-based messaging or misleading guarantees.
